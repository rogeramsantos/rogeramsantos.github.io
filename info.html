Portrait

Roger designs digital products that make complex things simple, with an engineer’s precision and a developer’s understanding of how software is built.

Moving into UX/UI is not a restart. For more than a decade in civil engineering and BIM, in Brazil and Germany, he turned complex building data into precise visual documentation and defined what information each partner needed, when, and in what structure. That is visual communication and information architecture, now applied to digital products.

With an M.Sc. in Digital Engineering and hands-on Python, C#, Java and SQL, he thinks in systems, states and data, and speaks the language of the developers who build what he designs.

↓ Based in Berlin. English, German, Portuguese.
