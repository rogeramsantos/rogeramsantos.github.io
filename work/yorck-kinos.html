Yorck Kinos

Helping new audiences discover, choose and book a film

ux research / service design / prototyping

Yorck runs some of Berlin’s best-loved independent cinemas. In a two-week industry-partner project, I researched how newer and younger audiences find Yorck online, pick a film, buy tickets and engage with Membership and Unlimited, then designed and tested a smoother path from discovery to seat.
